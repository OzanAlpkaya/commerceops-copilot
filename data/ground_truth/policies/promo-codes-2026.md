# Promotions and Campaign Codes 2026

Lumora Home · Issued 15 December 2025

## 1. How campaign codes work

Campaign codes give a percentage off the items in your order. Enter the code in the "Discount code" box at checkout; the discount is shown before you pay. The discount applies to the price of the items, not to the delivery charge.

Only one code can be used per order, and codes cannot be combined with each other. A code that is entered after the order has been placed cannot be applied to it. Codes have no cash value.
