# Shipping & Delivery Policy

Lumora Home · Last updated 16 February 2026

## 1. About this policy

This policy explains how we deliver orders, what delivery costs, what to do if a delivery arrives damaged or does not arrive, and what it costs to send a return back to us.

## 2. Order processing

We start preparing your order once the payment has been confirmed. When your order leaves our warehouse, we email you the carrier's name and the tracking number. Orders cannot be changed once they have been handed to the carrier.

We choose the carrier for each order based on the size and weight of the items. You cannot choose a carrier at checkout.

## 3. Delivery options

| Item type | Carrier | Service | Typical delivery time | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard parcel | Parcelo | Tracked parcel | 2–30 days after the order | €9.00–€60.00, by size and weight |
| Standard parcel | NordPost | Tracked parcel | 2–30 days after the order | €9.00–€60.00, by size and weight |
| Standard parcel | SwiftLane Express | Tracked parcel | 2–30 days after the order | €9.00–€60.00, by size and weight |
| Large item (over 20 kg) | Hollis Freight | Two-person delivery | 3–40 days after the order | €20.00–€270.00, by size and weight |

The delivery charge for your order is shown at checkout. The estimated delivery date on your order confirmation is the one to rely on.

## 4. Damaged or missing deliveries

Please check your delivery when it arrives. Report damage within 7 days of delivery, with photos of the item and its packaging. Keep the packaging until we have dealt with your report, as the carrier may ask to inspect it.

When we accept a damage report, we collect the damaged item free of charge and send a replacement or refund you, whichever you prefer.

If your order has not arrived by the estimated delivery date, contact Customer Care. We check the tracking with the carrier and, if the parcel cannot be found, send a replacement or refund you.

## 5. Return shipping costs

These costs apply to orders placed on or after 1 March 2026. For orders placed before that date, return shipping is free.

| Return reason | Parcel return | Collection (items over 20 kg) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Defective item | Free | Free |
| Wrong item sent | Free | Free |
| Damaged in transit | Free | Free |
| Change of mind | €4.95 | €29.00 |

The cost is deducted from your refund. If you exchange an item instead, we send the replacement at no extra delivery charge.

## 6. Large items and collection

Items over 20 kg are delivered and collected by Hollis Freight. Their two-person team brings the item to the room of your choice and removes the packaging. When you return a large item, we contact you to book a collection date; the item must be ready at ground-floor level and, where possible, in its original packaging.

## 7. Outlet

Outlet items are sold at reduced prices. They are delivered in the same way as other items, and the delivery options in §3 apply.

## 8. Tracking

Every order has a tracking number from the carrier, which you can use on the carrier's website. Tracking numbers look like this:

- Parcelo: PCL followed by 12 digits, for example PCL004417290315
- NordPost: NP, 9 digits and IE, for example NP521807364IE
- SwiftLane Express: SLX, four letters and six digits, for example SLX-KDRW-570214
- Hollis Freight: HF followed by 8 digits, for example HF73019264

Tracking can take up to one working day to update after we hand your order to the carrier.
