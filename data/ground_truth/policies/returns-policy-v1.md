# Returns Policy

Lumora Home · Last updated 1 June 2024

## 1. Scope

This policy explains how to return items bought from Lumora Home on our website. It covers returns when you change your mind, and what happens when an item arrives damaged, turns out to be defective or is not what you ordered.

Throughout this policy, "you" means the customer who placed the order and "we" means Lumora Home. The order date is the date shown on your order confirmation email.

## 2. Return window

You can return items within 30 days of delivery.
Defective, damaged or wrong items: see §5.

We cannot accept a change-of-mind return once the window has passed.

## 3. Condition of items

You do not need to keep items sealed to return them. An item can be returned opened as long as it is unused, clean, undamaged and complete with all its parts, accessories and instructions. Please use the original packaging where you can; if you no longer have it, pack the item so that it cannot move or break in transit.

Items that come back used, marked or damaged after delivery cannot be accepted, and we will send them back to you. Some items have special rules, set out in §5.

## 4. Refunds and exchanges

When you return an item, you can choose a refund or an exchange. An exchange is for the same item in a different size or colour, subject to stock.

Refunds are made to the original payment method and cover the full price of the returned items. Returning an item costs you nothing.

The original delivery charge is not refunded.

## 5. Special cases

### 5.1 Hygiene items

Opened pillows, bedding, mattresses and mattress protectors cannot be returned for hygiene reasons. The same applies to towels, bathrobes, mattress toppers and weighted blankets. Unopened items in their sealed packaging can be returned as normal.

### 5.2 Damaged items

If an item arrives damaged, please do not send it back straight away. See Shipping & Delivery Policy §4.

### 5.3 Defective items

If an item develops a fault, it is covered by our Warranty and Defects Policy, which sets out how long you have to report it and what we will do.

### 5.4 Wrong items

If we sent you a different item from the one you ordered, tell us within two weeks of delivery. We collect the wrong item free of charge and either send the right one or refund you, whichever you prefer.

## 6. How to return

1. Start a return from the Orders page in your account, or contact Customer Care with your order number.
2. Tell us which items you are returning and why. We confirm the return by email and give you a return number.
3. Pack the item securely and attach the returns label we send you. Large items are collected instead; we contact you to book a collection date.
4. Send the item back within 30 days of receiving it.
5. Keep your proof of postage until your refund or exchange is complete.

We inspect every returned item when it reaches our warehouse. We email you when the return has been received and again when the refund or exchange has been processed.

## 7. Refund timing

We process refunds within 10 working days of receiving the returned item at our warehouse. Your bank or card provider may take a few more days to show the money in your account.

If you paid by bank transfer, we refund to the account the payment came from. We cannot refund to a different account or in cash.

## 8. Contact

Customer Care answers questions about returns by email at support@lumorahome.example and by live chat on our website, Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:30. Please have your order number ready.
