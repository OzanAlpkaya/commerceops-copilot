# Warranty and Defects Policy

Lumora Home · Last updated 16 February 2026

## 1. Your statutory rights

This policy does not affect your statutory rights.

## 2. Defective items

Defective items can be returned for a replacement or refund within 30 days of delivery.

An item is defective when it does not work as described, has a manufacturing fault, or is missing parts that should have been in the box. Return shipping for defective items is free: we send you a returns label or, for large items, arrange a collection.

We replace the item with the same product where we have it in stock. If you prefer, or if the product is no longer available, we refund you instead. A replacement does not restart the period above.

## 3. Reporting a defect

Contact Customer Care with your order number, a short description of the fault and photos or a short video that show it. For furniture, include a photo of the product label, usually under the seat, inside a drawer or on the back panel.

We review every report within two working days. We may ask you to send the item back for inspection before we replace or refund it, or we may send a replacement part if the fault is limited to a part that is easy to fit.

## 4. Electrical appliances

If a kitchen appliance stops working, stops heating or trips your electricity, stop using it straight away and unplug it. Do not open the casing or try to repair the appliance yourself: this can make it unsafe.

When you report a fault with an appliance, tell us the model name from the rating plate, when the fault started and what the appliance does or does not do. Keep the plug, cable and any removable parts with the appliance when you return it.

## 5. What is not covered

This policy covers faults that were present when the item was delivered or that come from how it was made. It does not cover:

- normal wear and tear, such as fading or pilling of fabrics over time;
- damage caused by misuse, accidents, pets or not following the care instructions;
- natural variation in materials such as wood grain, marble veining and linen texture;
- items that have been altered, repaired or used for business purposes;
- consumable parts such as bulbs, filters and batteries, once they have been used.
